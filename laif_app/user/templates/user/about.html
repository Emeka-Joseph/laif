Our founders

Bishop Dr. Jonathan Njoku & Pastor Dr. Zinny Njoku

Life Abundant International Fountain (LAIF) Church was founded and formally established in 2010 in Kuwait, under the leadership of Bishop Dr. Jonathan Chukwuemeka Njoku as Founder and Senior Pastor, and his wife, Senior Pastor Dr. Zinny (Ezinne) Njoku, as Co-Founder.

Before establishing LAIF, Bishop Jonathan served faithfully under The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) for over 20 years, where he was ordained a Full Pastor and appointed Country Coordinator for RCCG Kuwait. In 2000 he pioneered the first African church congregation in Kuwait under RCCG, pastoring it for nearly ten years. Under divine direction, and after prayer and the blessing of his spiritual mentor, RCCG General Overseer Pastor E. A. Adeboye, he established LAIF Church in 2010.

Beyond ministry, Bishop Jonathan is a Professor of Accounting, Finance and Strategy Management at the American International University, Kuwait, holding a PhD, a Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) and M.Phil in Finance from the Maastricht School of Management, an MBA and B.Sc. from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, and Fellowships of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (FCA) and the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (FCTI).

Pastor Dr. Zinny is an ordained Full Pastor and Senior Pastor with a heart for discipleship, prayer, pastoral care, counselling and women's ministry. Professionally, she is an accomplished healthcare quality professional with over 25 years at Kuwait's Ministry of Health, holding a B.Sc., an MBA and a Certified Professional in Healthcare Quality (CPHQ) credential, alongside a PhD in Biblical Studies and Ministry.

What we believe

Our statement of faith

At LAIF Church, our doctrine is rooted in the infallible Word of God, the foundation of what we believe. We believe in one true God, revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; in the Lordship and saving grace of Jesus Christ; and in the transforming power of the Holy Spirit in the life of every believer.

We hold firmly that salvation is by grace through faith in Christ alone, and that the Church is the body of Christ, called to reflect His glory on earth. These core truths shape our identity and guide all that we stand for as a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled ministry.

We are committed to living lives of holiness, love and obedience to God — teaching sound biblical doctrine that leads to spiritual growth, discipleship and kingdom impact, and raising disciples who not only know the truth but live it out and pass it on to others.

Our journey

LAIF Church history

LAIF commenced with a small group of around 15 believers, facing the early challenges of a restricted religious environment. Through discipleship and commitment, the congregation grew steadily — developing structured training classes (Foundation, Membership and more), establishing departments such as Choir, Ushering and Welfare, hosting Annual Conventions, and building a strong cell group system.

Today, LAIF is recognised as a Bible-believing African Christian congregation serving members from diverse cultural and national backgrounds, with a vision for a future LAIF Head Quarters building project.

Where we are

Headquartered in Imo State, Nigeria, and officially registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) as a religious, non-profit organisation, with branches in Ghana, Kuwait and the United States of America. Worship services in Kuwait are held at the NECK Compound, Kuwait City, within the framework of Kuwait's laws — the church promotes respect for local law, peaceful coexistence and responsible citizenship among all congregants.

Cell groups & member care
